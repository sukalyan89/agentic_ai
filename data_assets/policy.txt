1. General Order Policy
Orders may be placed through authorized sales representatives, physical stores, or approved online channels. Every order must have a unique Order ID, such as REG100001.
- Both retail and wholesale customers may place orders.
- Orders are confirmed only after payment authorization.
- Customers may request cancellation before dispatch.
- Changes to quantity, product, or delivery address require approval before shipment.
- Wholesale orders involving 10 or more units may require additional verification.

2. Pricing, Discounts & Promotions
Product prices are determined by the applicable unit price, quantity, and approved discount.
- Discounts must be reflected in the final transaction amount.
- Promotional codes such as SAVE10, WINTER15, and FREESHIP are subject to eligibility.
- Multiple promotions cannot be combined unless explicitly authorized.
- Shipping charges may be billed separately.
- Pricing errors may be corrected before order acceptance, with customer notification.

3. Payment & Refund Policy
Accepted payment methods include credit cards, debit cards, cash, gift cards, and online payments.
- Payment must be completed before order dispatch unless credit terms have been approved.
- Refunds are normally processed to the original payment method.
- Approved refunds should be initiated within 7 business days.
- Gift-card payments are refunded as store credit unless applicable law requires otherwise.
- Suspected fraudulent payments may be held for investigation.

4. Delivery & Shipping Policy
Orders are shipped to the delivery address provided at checkout.
- Standard delivery target: 3–10 business days from dispatch.
- Delivery charges depend on product, location, and applicable promotions.
- FREESHIP may waive eligible shipping charges.
- Customers should report missing or visibly damaged packages within 48 hours.
- Delivery delays caused by logistics disruptions will be communicated where practical.

5. Return & Exchange Policy
Customers may request returns for eligible products within 15 calendar days of delivery.
- Products must generally be returned with their original accessories and packaging.
- Defective, damaged, or incorrectly supplied products qualify for review.
- Change-of-mind returns may be subject to return-shipping costs.
- Exchanges depend on inventory availability.
- Wholesale returns involving bulk quantities require manager approval.
- Statutory consumer rights take precedence over these conditions.

6. Customer Dispute Resolution
Customers may raise disputes relating to pricing, payments, deliveries, product quality, or refunds.
- Complaints should reference the Order ID.
- Customer support should acknowledge complaints within 2 business days.
- Initial investigations should be completed within 7 business days where possible.
- Unresolved disputes are escalated to the regional manager.
- Customers may pursue applicable external consumer remedies if internal resolution fails.

7. Customer Privacy & Data Protection
The company collects customer names, order information, payment-related records, and delivery information for legitimate business operations.
- Customer information must be accessed only by authorized personnel.
- Personal data must not be sold or shared for unrelated purposes without a valid legal basis.
- Payment information must be handled through secure, approved systems.
- Customer records must be retained only as necessary for operational, legal, or regulatory requirements.
- Customers may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information subject to applicable law.

8. Product Warranty & Quality
Products must meet applicable quality and safety standards.
- Manufacturer warranties apply where provided.
- Warranty claims may require proof of purchase and the Order ID.
- Damage caused by misuse, unauthorized modifications, or improper handling may be excluded from voluntary warranty coverage.
- Warranty remedies may include repair, replacement, or refund according to applicable terms and law.

9. Liability & Limitation of Responsibility
The company is responsible for fulfilling accepted orders with reasonable care.
- The company will investigate verified delivery errors and defective-product claims.
- Liability for eligible claims may be limited to the affected transaction value where legally permitted.
- The company is not responsible for losses caused solely by incorrect information provided by customers.
- Nothing in this policy excludes liability or consumer protections that cannot legally be excluded.

10. Sales Operations & Employee Responsibilities
Sales representatives must maintain accurate transaction records.
- Each transaction must identify the responsible salesperson.
- Unauthorized discounts, fabricated returns, and duplicate orders are prohibited.
- Regional managers supervise disputes, exceptional refunds, and operational compliance.
- Sales records must accurately reflect quantity, unit price, discounts, shipping costs, and final amounts.

11. Fraud Prevention
The company may review suspicious transactions to protect customers and business operations.
- Unusual bulk orders or repeated high-value purchases may trigger verification.
- Suspicious refund patterns must be escalated for review.
- Employees must not disclose customer payment information.
- Confirmed fraudulent activity may result in account restrictions or referral to relevant authorities.

12. Policy Exceptions & Updates
Exceptional circumstances may require approval from an authorized regional manager.
- Exceptions must be documented with the relevant Order ID.
- Policy changes must be communicated to affected teams.
- The company reserves the right to revise these policies prospectively, subject to applicable law and existing contractual obligations.
